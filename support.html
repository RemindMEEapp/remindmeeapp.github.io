My reminder did not ring

Allow notifications and alarms for RemindMEE in your phone settings. Keep the volume up, and do not swipe the app closed.

The date or details are wrong

AI can make mistakes. Check the details before saving. You can edit a reminder at any time.

Restore my purchase

Sign in to RemindMEE, open My Plan on the Reminders screen, tap Get more scans, then Restore purchases. "Get more scans" appears when 5 or fewer scans are left. Otherwise, email us.

Cancel my subscription

iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
Android: Google Play, then Subscriptions.

Delete my account

In the app, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of the home screen, then Delete account. This does not cancel a subscription. More details
