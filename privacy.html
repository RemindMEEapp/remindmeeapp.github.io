RemindMEE — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 8 October 2026
Last Updated: 27 November 2026

RemindMEE ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered reminder application operated by RemindMEE, based in India.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, store, and protect personal information when you use the RemindMEE application on Android and iPhone.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

We collect the email address or mobile phone number you provide when registering or signing in, along with account identifiers and authentication-related information. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google to create and identify your account. We do not receive your Google password.

On iPhone, you can choose to sign in with Apple. We receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address (which may be a private relay address that forwards to your real email). We do not receive your Apple Account password. We also keep a token issued by Apple, used only to remove the Sign in with Apple connection if you delete your account.

1.2 Photos, Screenshots, and Documents

When you scan a photograph or screenshot, RemindMEE processes the selected image to extract information that may help create a reminder.

Images may contain names, addresses, financial information, identification details, or other personal data.

Selected images are transmitted to our backend and AI-processing providers. Photos associated with saved reminders may also be stored locally and in your account's cloud storage.

1.3 Reminder Information

We process reminder titles, dates, times, categories, notes, and extracted information, including amounts, expiry dates, and other fields you save.

1.4 Device Permissions

RemindMEE may request camera, photo library, microphone, speech recognition, and notification or alarm permissions to provide relevant features. You can change these at any time in your device settings.

If you use voice input, your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on Android, usually provided by Google; on iPhone, provided by Apple) converts your speech into text. RemindMEE does not record or store audio. The text, or a date you type, may be sent to our backend and AI provider (Google Gemini) to understand the date and time; it is used only to fill in your reminder and is not stored separately.

RemindMEE does not request access to your phone's contact list. If you choose to add someone from your contacts (see section 1.6), the app uses your phone's own contact picker (Android or iPhone), which shares with RemindMEE only the single contact and phone number you select.

We do not currently use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

1.5 Purchase Information

Purchases are processed using Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iPhone), together with RevenueCat.

A subscription bought in one store is managed only in that store. Your scan allowance follows your RemindMEE account.

We receive information needed to verify purchases, manage subscriptions, and allocate scan credits, including purchase identifiers and subscription status.

We do not directly collect payment card details.

1.6 Notify Someone on WhatsApp (Optional)

If you turn on "Notify someone on WhatsApp?" for a reminder, we store with that reminder the people you choose (their name and phone number, picked from your contacts or typed by you), your message wording, and the time you last sent it. This information is part of your reminder, so it is backed up and synchronized to your account like your other reminder data, and it is removed when you delete the reminder or your account.

RemindMEE never sends WhatsApp messages on your behalf. When you tap a send button, the app opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and you decide whether to tap Send inside WhatsApp. Once you send, the message is handled by WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

If you switch on "Send my photo along with the message" for a reminder that has a photo, the app hands that photo to WhatsApp together with your message when you tap a send button. The photo goes from your phone directly to WhatsApp (it is not sent to us for this purpose), it is shared only if you switched this on, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send in WhatsApp. A temporary copy of the photo is kept in the app's private cache on your phone while it is being shared. Please share only photos you are entitled to share, as a photo may show other people or private details.

You should only add people whose phone numbers you are entitled to use for this purpose. Messages include your RemindMEE display name and a link to our app page, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not collect any information about the people who receive your messages or who open that link.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process information to:

  • Create, manage, and synchronize reminders.
  • Extract text and reminder details using OCR and AI.
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • Deliver or schedule reminders.
  • Prepare the WhatsApp messages you choose to send from your reminders.
  • Manage subscriptions, purchases, and scan allowances.
  • Prevent fraud, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the service.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your photographs or document contents to advertisers.

4. AI Processing and Accuracy

RemindMEE uses automated image recognition and artificial intelligence.

AI-generated reminder details may be inaccurate, incomplete, or incorrectly inferred.

Users must review and confirm important dates, times, amounts, and other information before relying on reminders.

RemindMEE's reminders and notifications are a support tool only. Do not rely solely on the app's alerting mechanism for critical legal or financial obligations — always maintain an independent check or backup reminder.

RemindMEE is not a substitute for professional legal or financial advice.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account information, reminders, and cloud-backed photographs are stored using Supabase.

Access controls are designed to restrict access to information associated with each authenticated user.

We apply reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee that any internet-based service is completely secure.

6. Photo and Reminder Deletion

When you delete an individual reminder, RemindMEE attempts to remove its associated photograph from your device and Supabase cloud storage.

Important limitations:

  • Cloud deletion requires successful communication with the backend.
  • If your device is offline or a cloud deletion request fails, the associated photograph may remain in cloud storage.
  • The current version does not guarantee automatic retries of failed cloud-photo deletions.
  • Removing a reminder does not necessarily mean that every associated copy, backup, or service-provider record has been immediately erased.

If you want to request deletion of remaining photographs or personal information, contact remindmee9999@gmail.com.

We will handle valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app (tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right of the home screen → Delete account), or by contacting remindmee9999@gmail.com.

Full account-deletion steps are also available on our web page: Delete Your Account.

Account deletion removes your account-associated cloud photographs, authentication records, and related application data (reminders, subscription records, usage history). If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove the Sign in with Apple connection.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, or on iPhone in Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Deletion may be subject to legal retention obligations, technical limitations, and backup-retention periods.

We will process valid deletion requests within applicable legal time limits.

8. Data Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, process purchases, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Reminder records and associated photographs may remain stored while an account exists.

Deleted reminders may have residual cloud photographs if deletion fails.

Some information may be retained in backups, transaction records, or service-provider systems according to applicable retention requirements.
